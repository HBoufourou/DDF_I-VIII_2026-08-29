## What is not currently derived

- a physical radius (R(t));
- an (8.2,mumathrm m) bulk thickness;
- a spin-2 Kaluza–Klein spectrum on a complete compactification;
- a globally consistent (mathcal N=1) orientifold vacuum;
- dark-matter, dark-energy or baryogenesis observables;
- seven physical charges and morphisms realizing a Fano plane;
- a stable BPS tower with a computed protected index.

These are research targets, not conclusions of the current evidence.

## Replacement publication plan

The corrected programme uses at most three independent tracks rather than
eight linked manuscripts:

1. **Paper G — geometry:** R63 plus a strictly bounded R64 proposition, after
   all local, toric, lattice, novelty and expert-review gates close.
2. **Paper KK — finite-neck spectral geometry:** begins only when R66 applies
   the R65 classifier to one real finite DDF geometry.
3. **Paper P — physical compactification and scale:** possible only after
   R67–R70 construct the operator dictionary, stabilized modulus, global
   consistency and scale hierarchy.

None of these papers is submission-ready on 5 September 2026.

## Citation policy

This repository may be cited for historical priority, failed routes or the
evolution of the programme. It should not be cited as evidence that the
micron-scale or phenomenological claims have been established.
